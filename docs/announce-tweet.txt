Burn Bar 2.0 for Omarchy.

I pay for Claude, Codex, Grok and Kimi. I could already see how much I burned on each one. What I couldn't see was which one I should be using right now. So I made the bar tell me.

Right now mine says USE GROK NEXT. I've used 3% of Grok's week and the week is half gone, so 49% of it is sitting in the bank. Claude says COME BACK IN 8:36:42 if I stop, because I burned 10% of the week in the first 5% of it. Codex is spent until Saturday afternoon. Kimi is on pace.

The banked number is a clock. Stay off a sub and you watch it climb. Get behind and you get a countdown to when you're even again.

If you only have one sub it keeps quiet, there's nothing to pick. It never suggests your local GPU. And it won't send you to a sub whose 5 hour window is about to fill up.

What else is in 2.0: one big card per sub with everything about it. A burndown graph on every card. Cards glow and you pick what the glow means. A SETUP page with every option on one screen. Nothing scrolls and it fits a 1080p laptop.

Before I shipped it I handed about 7,800 lines to Grok 4.6 and Kimi k3 and told them to find what I got wrong. 22 real bugs. The worst one: with Kimi and the GPU lane both turned on, the GPU lane was drawing right on top of Kimi. It was doing that on my own machine and I hadn't noticed. The whole list is in the repo, including the two findings I threw out and why.

My numbers today: 16.1M tokens in 24 hours, 2,404 turns, 75 sessions.

Omarchy is DHH's and Basecamp's. This is a plugin on top of it. MIT.
